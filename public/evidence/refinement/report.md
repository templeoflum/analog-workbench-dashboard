# Cadet VII numerical refinement study

Six partial signal-path configurations, 30 fresh runs. Same TI LM6172 library, ideal ±12 V rails, ideal offset (position 0.6), 499 Ω sources, 100 kΩ load, and 0–0.2 V input pulse as the earlier experiment. No hardware measurements.

## Method

Four explicit maximum timesteps: 0.1, 0.05, 0.025, 0.0125 ns. Fixed reltol=1e-6, abstol=1e-12, vntol=1e-9; KLU and GMIN=1e-16 throughout. Fifth run keeps 0.0125 ns and tightens all three tolerances tenfold. Export precision increased to 15 digits. Differences are the maximum absolute output difference on the union of both adaptive sample grids, linearly interpolating the other trace, over the whole 0–500 ns interval (both pulse edges). This includes interpolation effects and is not an error bound against an exact solution.

All 30 runs completed. All AC and transient outputs have finite values and strictly increasing axes; transient traces reach 500 ns. Ten runs (input-offset gain endpoints, all five settings) retain dynamic-gmin warnings and complete through true-gmin fallback. These are not warning-free fixtures.

## Observations

Successive timestep differences decrease in all six cases. Worst differences across configurations: 0.179867, 0.056718, 0.015600 mV. At the finest timestep, tightening tolerances changes the result by at most 0.002005 mV. These observations support diminishing numerical sensitivity in this particular sweep. No pass threshold or physical accuracy claim is assigned. Previous mixed-settings experiment remains preserved separately.

## Repeat

Run `python3 study.py` with ngspice 47 (KLU enabled) installed. Script uses paths relative to itself, preserves netlists/logs, rejects errors and invalid data, and writes refinement.json. Each subprocess has a 45-second timeout. run_cadet.py supplies the inherited circuit generator; invoke study.py for this experiment. The unchanged TI model and its redistribution notice are included. Raw waveforms are retained locally and regenerated by the downloadable fixture; not included in the download.
